<task type="auto" id="01-01-02">
<title>Implement @ghostpilot/shared Package</title>
<instruction>
Create `packages/shared` with `package.json`, `tsconfig.json`, and source files:
1. `src/types/index.ts`:
   - `UserProfile`: id, email, fullName, avatarUrl, creditsBalance, planTier ('free' | 'pro' | 'enterprise'), createdAt, updatedAt
   - `InterviewSession`: id, userId, title, mode ('interview' | 'general_call' | 'mock'), language, customInstructions, status ('active' | 'completed' | 'archived'), saveTranscript, createdAt
   - `DocumentVaultItem`: id, userId, name, filePath, fileType ('pdf' | 'docx'), extractedText, createdAt
   - `TranscriptItem`: id, sessionId, userId, speaker ('interviewer' | 'candidate'), content, timestamp, createdAt
   - `AIResponseItem`: id, sessionId, userId, question, answer, responseType ('bullet_points' | 'full_answer' | 'code_solution'), createdAt
   - `CreditTransaction`: id, userId, amount, transactionType ('credit_purchase' | 'session_usage' | 'mock_interview_usage'), referenceId, createdAt
   - `DesktopAuthPayload`: token, user, expiresAt
2. `src/schemas/index.ts`:
   - Zod schemas validating all types above: `userProfileSchema`, `interviewSessionSchema`, `documentVaultItemSchema`, `transcriptItemSchema`, `aiResponseSchema`, `desktopAuthPayloadSchema`.
   - Zod environment variable schemas: `webEnvSchema`, `desktopEnvSchema`.
3. `src/index.ts`: Export all types and schemas.
</instruction>
<verification>
Ensure `packages/shared` compiles with TypeScript without errors and exports all required domain types and Zod schemas.
</verification>
</task>
