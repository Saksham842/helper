# Plan 03-01: Native Loopback & Microphone Audio Capture Engines

## Objective
Configure Electron main session media request handlers to silently approve system loopback audio capture (`audio: 'loopback'`) and microphone capture without prompt dialogs or OS recording banners, providing uninterrupted caller and candidate audio streams to the desktop application.

## Requirements Covered
- **AUDIO-01**: Native loopback audio capture (WASAPI Loopback on Windows / CoreAudio on macOS) grabs caller/interviewer voice without browser tab-sharing banners.
- **AUDIO-02**: Microphone capture grabs candidate speech independently on a separate audio stream.

## Tasks
1. Update `apps/desktop/src/main/index.ts` / `apps/desktop/src/main/hud.ts`:
   - Configure `session.defaultSession.setDisplayMediaRequestHandler` to automatically resolve screen/loopback requests silently without popping OS screen-share modals.
   - Configure `session.defaultSession.setPermissionRequestHandler` to auto-approve microphone access for `apps/desktop`.
2. Create `apps/desktop/src/renderer/src/services/audioCapture.ts`:
   - `captureLoopbackAudio()`: Calls `navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia` with `chromeMediaSource: 'desktop'` audio constraint.
   - `captureMicrophoneAudio()`: Calls `navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia` with echo cancellation, noise suppression, and auto gain control.
   - Device selector utility to list available microphones and playback loopback sources.
   - Stream lifecycle management (start, stop, mute, audio level metrics).
3. Expose audio device queries and capture status through the preload bridge if needed.

## Verification
- Verify `setDisplayMediaRequestHandler` executes and grants loopback audio access without user prompt.
- Confirm both loopback and microphone MediaStreams initialize cleanly with active audio tracks.
